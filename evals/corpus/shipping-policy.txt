Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days within the country and is free on orders over $50.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs an additional $15. International shipping is
available to select countries and takes 10 to 20 business days depending on customs processing.
We are unable to ship to P.O. boxes for express or international orders. Once an order ships, you
will receive a tracking link by email; tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to first appear.
