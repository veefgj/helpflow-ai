Account Deletion Policy

Users can permanently delete their account from the Account Settings page under "Delete my
account". Deletion takes effect immediately for login purposes, but the account can still be
recovered within 30 days by contacting support. After 30 days the account cannot be recovered.
All personal data associated with a deleted account is fully purged from our systems within 90
days, in line with our data retention policy. Billing records are retained for 7 years as required
by tax law, even after account deletion.
