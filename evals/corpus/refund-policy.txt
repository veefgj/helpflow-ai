Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked.
After 30 days, refunds are issued as store credit only and cannot be returned to the original
payment method. Enterprise customers on annual contracts have an extended 60 day refund window
instead of the standard 30 days. Refund requests must be submitted through the billing portal;
refunds are processed within 5 business days of approval. Shipping fees are non-refundable except
when the return is due to a defect or a shipping error on our part.
